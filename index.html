Using it on an iPad

  • Open the page address in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. After that, always open the workbook from the home screen icon so her progress stays in one place.
  • Progress is saved on the iPad, inside the home screen icon. Opening the same address in Safari starts a separate copy, and deleting the icon erases her progress.

How the lessons work

  • Each lesson starts with a short Learn box, then two warm-up problems from the last lesson, then new practice.
  • A lesson is finished when every problem is correct. Wrong answers turn orange so she can fix them and check again.
  • The next lesson opens after the one before it is finished.
  • Every lesson has an answer key at the bottom.
